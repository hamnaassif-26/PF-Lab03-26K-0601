*Algorithm of finding even and odd numbers*
**Start** the program.
**Input** the integer number from user.
**Divide** the number by 2 and check the remainder by using **modulus** operator (number % 2).
**Check** if remainder is equals to 0.
**If True** display The number is even and go to step 7.
**If False** display The number is odd and go to step 7.
**Stop** the program.
